PROPOSTA 1
Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished professional brand identity concept board for ELOCIVA, premium enterprise municipal management software sold to Brazilian city governments. It integrates administration, digital citizen services, business and service provider access, automation. This is a serious high-value procurement brand. No healthcare imagery or words, no crosses, no governmental seals, no stock skylines, no handshakes, no photoreal objects. Beautiful restrained Swiss editorial design, impeccable Portuguese typography, generous space, flat vector-like graphics. Landscape presentation board with a very large hero logo and tagline upper area, color swatches with hex labels, small monochrome brand reproduction, and lower area a convincing small municipal software interface application. Clear hierarchy, intentional grid, readable sparse copy, no excessive tiny text. Exact brand spelling ELOCIVA. Primary tagline "Gestão integrada. Município conectado." Descriptor "Plataforma de gestão municipal". UI example labels "Visão do município", "Serviços digitais", "Cidadão", "Empresas", "Gestão". Put small "Estudo conceitual" footer. Each design must feel like a crafted premium identity, not a template collage. Direction title "01 / INSTITUCIONAL CONTEMPORÂNEA". Evolution of an existing navy and cobalt brand with geometric E monogram: create a highly distinctive solid geometric E built of three purposeful horizontal rounded bars and a connecting spine, carefully balanced, no health motif. All-uppercase ELOCIVA in sophisticated medium-weight geometric sans with controlled spacing. Palette navy #172F50, cobalt #2856B8, pale blue #CBDEFA, offwhite #F4F6FA. Mostly navy hero with white wordmark, clean offwhite remainder. Quiet confidence, civic infrastructure, stability. The brand symbol should be large enough to evaluate.
