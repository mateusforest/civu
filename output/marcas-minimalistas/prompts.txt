01-civa
Use case: logo-brand. Design an exceptionally refined MINIMAL logo concept presentation for a Brazilian municipal software brand. The product unifies the entire municipality's administration, automates processes, and gives citizens, companies and service providers digital access. Audience is serious high-value government enterprise buyers, but the identity should be accessible, memorable and effortlessly simple.
USER FEEDBACK: Previous proposals felt too elaborate and names too long. New direction: short, easily pronounced two-syllable name, clean design with real personality, minimal and unforgettable.
Produce one landscape graphic approximately 1536x1024. Absolutely flat solid colors on pure white, crisp vector-like finish, generous whitespace, exquisite typography, polished professional brand design. NOT a busy brand board. NO dashboard, photos, texture, shadows, 3D, gradients, giant background motif, large collage, paragraphs, or long brand story.
LAYOUT: tiny discreet numbered name label top left; center-left to center large main logo lockup with a simple distinctive symbol on the left and a bespoke lowercase wordmark; modest small descriptor beneath wordmark "Gestão municipal integrada". Main logo at least 900 pixels wide overall with ample room to breathe. One very short tagline placed below with considerable whitespace, restrained typography. Along bottom, an understated thin divider, two small color chips at left with hex codes, at right a SMALL black-only reproduction of exact same symbol and one SMALL rounded-square app icon with exact same symbol. Tiny footer "Estudo de marca". Maintain identical mark geometry in all reproductions. Typography and silhouette are the entire personality. Brand name MUST be immediately legible. Design symbol in ONE simple continuous silhouette or at most TWO solid shapes, and make it work in black at 16 pixels. No healthcare cross, plus sign, coat of arms, government seal, flags, skyline, network nodes, infinity loop, generic chain link, or handshake. No ELOCIVA anywhere. Do not invent any extra text.
BRAND: "civa", pronounced CÍ-va, draws on civic life and city citizenship. A compact approachable confident municipal tech identity.
SYMBOL: an original lowercase c-inspired OPEN CIRCLE with squared inner opening: one thick slightly squarish soft-corner C silhouette, one tiny precise diagonal cut at each open terminal gives recognizable forward orientation. The opening is clearly on the RIGHT. The shape must unequivocally read C and open access, never G, power button, euro symbol or medical emblem. No detached dot and no enclosing outer badge in the main lockup.
WORDMARK: "civa" in expertly spaced custom soft geometric lowercase sans. The dot on i is a subtle rounded square echoing symbol geometry; a single-storey a. Strong enough for procurement presentations but friendly on a citizen app, not childish. Pure deep midnight #172D36 for the wordmark, muted vivid teal #168779 for the main symbol. Two colors only plus white.
Exact top label "01 / CIVA".
Exact tagline "A cidade conectada."
Lower app icon midnight field with white symbol. The black-only symbol should use pure black.
